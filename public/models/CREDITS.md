# 3D model credits

All anatomy models below are from **NIH 3D** (https://3d.nih.gov), part of the
**HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas (HRA) 3D Reference Organ** set, derived from the
U.S. National Library of Medicine **Visible Human Male** dataset, authored by
**Kristen Browne & Heidi Schlehlein**. All are licensed **CC-BY 4.0** (attribution
required). They share one coordinate space, so they register in correct anatomical
position when loaded together.

Notes:
- Brain geometry derives from the Allen Human Reference Atlas (Ding et al., 2016),
  packaged as an HRA reference organ.
- Visible Human source bibliography: Spitzer et al. 1996; Ackerman 1998.
- kidney.glb is the right kidney; the lab mirrors it to render a pair.
- heart.glb was previously a Public-Domain calibration model (3DPX-022787); it was
  replaced by the HRA Male heart so it co-registers with the other organs.
